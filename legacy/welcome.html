Know where your money goes, in ringgit and sen.

VinExpenses tracks your spending and income, reads your receipts, keeps your budgets honest, and reminds you before a fixed deposit matures or a loan payment is due.

Open the app See what it does

Works in your browser on phone and computer. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

Everything in one calm place

Built for everyday use: add an expense in a few seconds, and see the month at a glance.

1

Scan a receipt

Take a photo or upload a PDF. The total, merchant and date are filled in for you to check. PDF receipts with text are read on your own device and never uploaded.

2

Budgets that warn you early

Set a monthly limit overall or per category. See what is safe to spend today, and get a warning before you go over.

3

Recurring bills and income

Rent, utilities, subscriptions and salary are added automatically when they fall due, so the month is never missing a bill.

4

Fixed deposit calculator

Enter the amount, rate and tenure to see what you will receive, with a daily, monthly or yearly breakdown. Save it to be reminded before it matures.

5

Loan calculator

Flat rate or reducing balance: see the monthly payment, the total interest, the real yearly rate and the full repayment schedule.

6

Reports you can keep

A monthly report compared with last month, printable or saved as PDF, and a spreadsheet export of everything whenever you want it.

Plan before you sign

Compare a bank's offer in seconds, then track it once you take it.

  • The answer updates as you type, with the three numbers that matter first.
  • A flat-rate loan shows its real yearly rate, so two offers can be compared fairly.
  • Add a loan to your recurring expenses and its payments stop by themselves after the last one.
  • Reminders by Telegram or in the app: 7 days and 1 day before a deposit matures, 3 days before a payment.

The calculators give estimates for a fixed rate. They are not financial advice, and your bank's figures can differ slightly.

Works where you are

  • Offline: open the app and add expenses without a connection; they sync when you are back online.
  • Telegram: connect the bot and add an expense by chatting, for example "lunch 12.50".
  • Phone and computer: one account, the same data on every device, in light or dark mode.
  • English interface, Malaysian receipts: the receipt reader understands English, Malay and Chinese totals.

Your data stays yours

Money is personal. These are facts about how the app is built, not promises for later.

No ads, no tracking

There are no advertising or analytics scripts. The app loads its code from this site only.

Only you can see your records

Every record is tied to your account and protected by database rules, so one account cannot read another's data.

Take it with you

Export everything as a spreadsheet at any time, from Account in the app.

Delete it for good

Delete your account from inside the app and your records are removed from the server.

The details, including the services that help run the app, are in the Privacy Policy.
