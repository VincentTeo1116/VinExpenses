Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

VinExpenses ("the app") is a free personal expense tracker run by Vincent Teo, an individual in Malaysia ("I", "me"). This policy explains what personal data the app handles, why, who else receives it, and what you can do about it. It is written to follow Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

The short version

  • The app stores what you type into it (your account email and your money records) so it can show them back to you. Nothing else is collected about you.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your data is not sold or used for marketing.
  • A few outside services help run the app. Each receives only what its job needs, listed in section 4.
  • You can export everything, and you can delete your account and its records from inside the app.

2. How your data is used

  • To run your account and show your records, totals, charts, reports and reminders.
  • To read a receipt you scan and suggest its total, merchant, date and category.
  • To send messages you asked for: account verification and password reset emails, the monthly report, and Telegram replies and reminders.
  • To keep the service safe: daily limits on scans and messages, and investigating misuse.

Your data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training artificial intelligence models by me, and it is not sold. I process it because you asked for the service by creating an account (your consent), and you can withdraw that consent by deleting your account.

3. What is stored on your device

The app does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It keeps the following in your browser's storage so that it works:

  • your sign-in session, so you stay logged in;
  • a copy of your records, so the app opens without a connection, and any entries waiting to be sent;
  • small preferences such as the colour theme and the last screen you used.

Logging out removes the copy of your records from that device. PDF receipts that contain text are read on your device and are not uploaded.

5. How long it is kept

  • Your account and records are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Deleting your account in the app (Account → Delete account) removes your account and the records linked to it from the server. Copies may remain in the provider's backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.
  • Receipt images are not stored after they are read. Daily usage counters are kept only to enforce limits.
  • An account that has been unused for a long time may be deleted after a notice is sent to its email address.

6. How it is protected

  • Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database rules restrict every record to the account that owns it.
  • The app loads its code from this site only, and server keys are never sent to your browser.

No online service can promise perfect security. Use a strong, unique password, keep your device locked, and tell me at once if you think your account has been accessed by someone else. If a breach affects your data, I will inform you without undue delay.

7. Your rights and choices

  • See and correct: everything you entered is visible and editable in the app.
  • Take a copy: Account → Your data → Export all (CSV).
  • Delete: delete single records, or delete your account in the app.
  • Limit: the optional features (receipt scanning, Nearby, Telegram, monthly email, AI help) are off until you use or enable them, and can be switched off again.
  • Ask or complain: write to the address below. I will answer within 21 days. You may also contact Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

8. Children

The app is intended for people aged 18 or older. If you are under 18, use it only with the permission of a parent or guardian, who agrees to this policy on your behalf. If you believe a child has created an account without permission, write to me and it will be removed.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the new date will be shown at the top and a notice will be shown in the app or sent by email before the change takes effect.
