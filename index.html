          Scan a receiptPhoto or PDF. PDFs with text are read on your device and never uploaded; photos go to OCR.space and aren't saved. Always review the draft.
          Tips for a clean scan
          • PDF receipts (e-mailed receipts, e-invoices) work best: exact, instant, nothing uploaded.
          • Photos: lay the receipt flat, in bright even light, with the whole receipt in the frame.
          • Avoid shadows, folds and glare over the total.
          • If the total is wrong, tap the right amount under the scan.
